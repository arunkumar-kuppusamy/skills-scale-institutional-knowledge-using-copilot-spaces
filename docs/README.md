# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management Documentation Center. These guides standardize how we run projects across the organization, from initiation through retrospectives.

## OctoAcme Project Management Approach

OctoAcme follows a structured, phased project management model designed to deliver customer value through iterative, quality-focused execution. The approach begins with **initiation**, where the team validates business needs, identifies stakeholders, and creates a one-pager capturing the problem statement, goals, success metrics, risks, and resource requirements. Once approved, the team enters **planning**, where the backlog is prioritized, milestones are mapped, dependencies are identified, and a definition of done is established. This phase ensures work is broken into manageable, testable increments.

The **execution** phase brings the plan to life through daily standups, sprint cycles, and disciplined pull request workflows. Quality is built in—unit tests, integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests, and security scanning are run in CI before code is merged. Teams track progress on project boards, monitor risks weekly, and escalate blockers through a clear escalation path (team → PM → Product Lead → Sponsor). **Deployment** and **release** processes are standardized to minimize risk, with pre-release checklists, smoke tests, rollback plans, and post-deploy verification.

Finally, the team conducts **retrospectives** after each sprint or milestone to capture learnings and convert them into action items. This continuous improvement mindset, combined with clear role definitions, transparent communication, and data-driven decisions, enables OctoAcme to deliver reliably on commitments while fostering a culture of learning and psychological safety.

## Core Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named Project Manager and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning

## Key Roles

- **Project Manager (PM)**: Coordinates delivery, manages schedules, risks, and communications
- **Product Manager (PdM)**: Defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success
- **Developers**: Implement features, collaborate on design and testability, identify technical risks
- **QA/Testing**: Validate quality and acceptance criteria
- **Stakeholders**: Provide input, approvals, and business context

## Communication Cadence

- **Daily**: Team standups (15 min) — focus on progress, blockers, dependencies
- **Weekly**: PM + PdM sync and stakeholder status updates
- **Sprint/Milestone**: Planning, demos, and reviews
- **Post-sprint/release**: Retrospectives to capture learnings

## How These Docs Connect

These documents work together to provide a complete project management framework:
- **Overview** sets the context and principles
- **Initiation** through **Planning** guides setup and scope definition
- **Execution & Tracking** and **Risk Management** support day-to-day delivery
- **Release & Deployment** ensures quality handoff to production
- **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement** closes the loop and feeds back into future projects

All guidance is grounded in the roles and personas defined in **Roles & Personas**, ensuring clarity about who does what and how cross-functional teams collaborate.
